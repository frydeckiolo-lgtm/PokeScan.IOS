# 🃏 PokeScan v2.5.0

**PokeScan** is an advanced, privacy-focused iOS application built with SwiftUI, designed specifically for Pokémon TCG collectors, hobbyists, and investors. Seamlessly bridge the gap between physical card collecting and digital portfolio tracking.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Instant Card Recognition:** Powered by Apple Vision and advanced OCR technology to scan cards in real-time or from your photo library.
* **Real-Time Market Pricing:** Integrated with live marketplace data (including eBay tracking) to deliver up-to-date pricing trends, history, and alerts.
* **Advanced Collection Management:** Organize your portfolio, track total estimated value, monitor ROI statistics, and filter cards by rarity, condition, or custom tags.
* **PikAI Assistant:** An intelligent multimodal AI companion to answer questions about card authenticity, grading guidelines, and collecting strategies.
* **PokeScan Collector (Mini-Game):** A fun digital pack-opening simulator where you can unbox virtual boosters, collect graded cards (PSA 4 to PSA 10 Gem Mint), complete daily challenges, and trade with friends using secure QR codes.
* **Privacy-First:** All personal collection data and history remain securely stored locally on your device.

## 📥 Download & Installation Guide

Because this app is distributed outside the official App Store, you will need to use a sideloading tool to install the `.ipa` file on your iOS device.

### Prerequisites
* An iOS device (iPhone/iPad) running a compatible iOS version.
* A computer (Mac/Windows/Linux) or a direct sideloading utility on your device.

### Method 1: Using Sideloadly or AltStore (Recommended)
1. Download the **`PokeScan.ipa`** file from the assets below.
2. Download and open **Sideloadly** (or AltStore) on your computer.
3. Connect your iPhone to your computer via USB and trust the device.
4. Drag and drop the `PokeScan.ipa` file into Sideloadly.
5. Enter your Apple ID credentials (a burner/free developer account works fine).
6. Click **Start** to install the app onto your phone.
7. On your iPhone, go to **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**, tap your Apple ID, and **Trust** the developer certificate.
8. Open PokeScan and enjoy!
